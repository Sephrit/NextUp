## Known issues — record what you do not fix

This repo keeps an `ISSUES.md`. **Read it before you start.** It lists the
defects and traps already known here, so you neither re-report one nor walk into
one a previous session already mapped. No file means this repo has not been
swept yet — create it when you have something to record.

Anything you find and do **not** fix goes in it before your session ends, even
when you found it in passing and it has nothing to do with the task you were
given. A finding that was only mentioned in conversation is a finding that is
gone tomorrow. Fixing an entry means deleting it in the same commit as the fix.

Format, severities, and what does not belong there:
`~/Developer/ISSUES-STANDARD.md`. Whole-machine view: `aporia open`.
